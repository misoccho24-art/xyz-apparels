About us

Welcome to Sakib Apparels

Sakib Apparels Ltd, established in 2016, is a renowned garment manufacturer committed to providing the best quality knitwear items such as polos, basic T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, trousers, swimsuits, tops, crop tops and kids' items.

Our factory is situated in Hemayetpur, Savar, Dhaka, Bangladesh.
